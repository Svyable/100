Engines, not one-offs.

WHAT COMPUTATION CAN AND CANNOT DO

Five modalities.

Every problem carries an editorial modality recording whether a finite object could refute or settle it.

ModalityMeaningProblemsWith verifier
Finitely refutableOne finite, checkable object would refute it; computation can search and certify ranges.1515
Finite parameterAsks for one specific finite value or object; exhaustive search or a certificate can settle it.22
Existence familyExistence for infinitely many finite parameters; constructions certify cases, one certified nonexistence refutes.22
AsymptoticLimiting or infinitary statement; computation gives data and certified bounds, never a refutation.161
StructuralAbout infinite or continuous objects; computation helps only through restricted finite analogues.650
