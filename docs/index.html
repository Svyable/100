03 / OUR STANDARD OF EVIDENCE

No breakthroughs
by announcement.

A proof has to establish the stated theorem, survive independent scrutiny, and leave behind enough detail to reproduce the claim. That is the whole point.

Read the research protocol ↗
01

Specify

Exact quantifiers, exceptions, source, and the frontier of known results.

02

Attack

Constrain a lemma, construct an example, or search for a falsifying instance.

03

Verify

Independent checking of mathematical meaning, not merely syntactic validity.

04

Compound

Preserve reusable tools and generalize verified results to the next problem.

04 / RANKING TRANSPARENCY

How we prioritize.

Four editorial factors, each scored 1–5. Ranking changes require a reason and a source; an exciting proof claim does not automatically change evidence status.

Mathematical impact30%
Attackability30%
Independent verifiability25%
Method spillover15%

Score = 6 × impact + 6 × attackability + 5 × verifiability + 3 × spillover
