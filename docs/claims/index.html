Hilbert's tenth problem over Q

Hilbert's tenth problem over the rational numbers ↗

Claimed
No algorithm decides whether an arbitrary integer-coefficient polynomial, with the number of variables part of the input, has a rational zero.
Scope
Matches the registry statement This is the same rational-field decision problem, not the already-settled integer version or undecidability for proper subrings of Q.
Validation
No independent validation established
Caveats
Unrefereed manuscript; proof depends on companion pointwise elliptic 2-converse, valuation and height arguments. No independent line-by-line verification located.
Next check
Verify the reduction from integer to rational decision tests and audit each cited arithmetic input, especially the October 7 pointwise 2-converse.

Scope

The (3,4,∞) modular-family construction of a complex structure on S6 ↗

Claimed
A compact complex threefold constructed from a family of complex two-tori and three special fibers is diffeomorphic to the standard smooth six-sphere.
Scope
Matches the registry statement If the gluing, complex smoothness and identification with the standard S6 all hold, this is a full positive answer to the registry's integrable-complex-structure question.
Validation
Independent reconstruction preprint (unrefereed)
Caveats
Philip Engel's September 29 independent 25-page self-contained proof is substantive corroborating research, not a recorded refereed acceptance or two independent specialist reviews. Reconcile claimed geometry with Campana–Demailly–Peternell constraints.
Next check

Kaplansky direct-finiteness conjecture

A torsion-free group algebra that is not directly finite ↗

Claimed
For a finitely presented torsion-free group G, there exist a,b in F_2[G] with ab=1 but ba != 1.
Scope
Matches the registry statement One valid characteristic-two example would refute the universal direct-finiteness claim; no result for every field is needed for a counterexample.
Validation
No independent validation established
Caveats
The construction is large and has not been independently reproduced in this audit; underlying group presentation and algebra calculations are central proof obligations.
Next check

M049 · PREPRINT 2026-10-04

Soficity conjecture

A torsion-free group algebra that is not directly finite ↗

Claimed
The constructed finitely presented torsion-free group is nonsofic; its group ring over F_2 is not directly finite.
Scope
Matches the registry statement A finitely presented nonsofic group is countable and refutes the universal soficity conjecture. The claimed nonsoficity may follow from the direct-finiteness obstruction, but is not an independently checked example.
Validation
No independent validation established
Caveats
Shares one manuscript and underlying construction with M044; do not count these as two independent proof sources. Soficity inference depends on correct direct-finiteness obstruction and the sofic-group finiteness theorem. The upstream author-described Lean comparator does not assert that the group is nonsofic; that conclusion needs a separate mathematical audit.
Next check
Independently verify M044's group algebra witness, then audit the Elek–Szabó implication from soficity to direct finiteness in the claimed coefficient field.

M098 · PREPRINT 2026-10-10

Vaught conjecture

A counterexample to Vaught's Conjecture for L_{omega_1,omega} ↗

Claimed
A sentence of infinitary L_{omega_1,omega} has exactly aleph_1 countable models (and no perfect set of non-isomorphic models).
Scope
A different statement M098 asks about complete first-order theories in a countable language, not infinitary L_{omega_1,omega} sentences. This manuscript does NOT refute or solve M098.
Validation
Formalised, but a different statement
Caveats
The pinned Lean development proves a thin aleph-1 spectrum for its own infinitary sentence, baseLanguage.densitySentence. The authors say this is not the first-order Vaught conjecture, that the formalization does not cover every claim of the manuscript, and that it does not identify those models with the manuscript's strongly coded templates. Knight's 2002 first-order announcement was not accepted.
Next check
Keep M098 verified_open. Record further first-order progress in research/M098_VAUGHT.md. A later revision of the infinitary formalization is not a resolution of this ID.
