FROM QUESTIONS TO EVIDENCE

Proof campaigns.

Cohort jobs, active research tracks with bounded proof obligations and falsifiers, and the shared attack shapes behind them. Ordered by dispatch: what to do next.

0Restricted theorem verified

Passing a small-case test is a verified computation, not a proof of an unrestricted conjecture. Stages advance only on reproduced or independently reviewed evidence.

ONE JOB, MANY PROBLEMS

Do the cohort, not the next vertex.

Each card is one action on one shared attack shape; the problems on it move together. Attack shapes and scheduling bonuses. A shape is not a theorem, not a claim that the case is open, and not a change to editorial scores or readiness.
