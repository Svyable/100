RESEARCH DOSSIERS

M006 — an explicit least-Goldbach-prime bound on the growth mine

Dated: 2026-11-01. Scope: every even integer $4 \le n \le 20000$. Not claimed: an asymptotic theorem, a bound through $4\cdot10^{18}$, or any progress toward the strong Goldbach conjecture beyond this finite window.

The overlooked step#

The certified mine goldbach_growth lists the twelve record-setting least Goldbach primes through $20000$, the largest being $173$ at $n=7426$. The campaign asked to test an explicit upper bound against those records, not to push the verified range toward the published $4\cdot10^{18}$ check.

Finite theorem#

For every even integer $n$ with $4 \le n \le 20000$, if $p$ is the least prime such that $n-p$ is prime, then

$$ p \le \lfloor 3(\ln n)^2\rfloor. $$

The independent checker rebuilds each least prime with an odd-only sieve and recomputes the same inequality. Because the right-hand side is increasing for $n\ge3$, it is enough that the bound dominate the twelve running-maximum records; the mine nevertheless verifies every even $n$ in range.

The sharpest ratio on the records is $173/(\ln 7426)^2 \approx 2.178$, so the constant $3$ has slack. Tightening the constant is cosmetic inside this window; exporting the inequality past $20000$ is refused by the dispatcher.

Evidence boundary#

  • Oliveira e Silva, Herzog and Pardi (Math. Comp. 2014) already verify Goldbach through $4\cdot10^{18}$. This note only shapes the tiny initial segment into an explicit inequality.
  • Do not promote M006 toward Solved.
  • Run:
python3 scripts/mine.py --mine goldbach_bound
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_mines.py' -v
