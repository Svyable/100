RESEARCH DOSSIERS

M076 — sharp ten-deletion stability of the 54-set sunflower family

Date: 2026-11-02 UTC. Claim: #220. Work ID: X-M076-F43-TEN-FIVE. Scope SHA-256: ab92a6f1f2243e7be174b8eadd16105947d0542f92a71ffc1d8587cf750ad295.

Classification: author-derived exact restricted theorem candidate. The certificate combines a C++ exhaustive cover-superset scanner, a Python independently represented literal-set replay, and a separate all-triples verification of an explicit witness. Both implementations are by the same author; no independent mathematical or historical-novelty review has occurred. This does not improve a global sunflower bound or settle the Erdős–Rado conjecture.

Precise statement#

Let U={0,...,17} be partitioned into two nine-point components. Each component consists of three three-point blocks. Let F contain, in each component, all four-sets taking two points from each of two distinct blocks. Thus |F|=54. A three-petal sunflower consists of three distinct sets with identical pairwise intersections, including the empty core.

Ten-deletion theorem candidate. For every ambient set V⊇U, every D⊆F with |D|≤10, and every family of distinct new four-sets I⊆binom(V,4)\\F, if (F\\D)∪I is sunflower-free, then

|I| ≤ 5.

Sharp witness at exactly ten deletions. Delete these indices from scripts.m076_f43_stability.base_family():

D = {0,2,3,5,6,8,18,24,25,26}.

Insert the five sets (all using one outside point, 18):

{0,1,3,18}, {0,2,3,18}, {1,2,3,18},
{3,6,7,18}, {5,6,7,18}.

The resulting 49-set family has no sunflower among its binom(49,3)=18,424 triples. Each of the ten deletions is essential for this particular five-insertion witness: restoring any one old set produces a sunflower. The preceding nine-deletion theorem candidate in M076_F43_NINE.md excludes five insertions after nine deletions; thus the first five-insertion threshold is exactly ten, conditional on that independently unreviewed nine-deletion result. No 55-set construction is obtained: after ten deletions one would need at least eleven insertions to improve 54.

Complete finite upper-bound certificate#

  1. Ambient reduction and 15 orbit exclusions. A new four-set is classified by its number of outside points and its sorted occupancy counts in the three blocks of each nine-point component, up to exchange of components. The 18 types are exhaustive even for arbitrarily many outside labels, because a single candidate has at most four. For 15 types, the forbidden-pair graph on the 54 old members contains 11 vertex-disjoint edges, each rechecked using literal set intersections. Ten deletions cannot cover these edges. The only surviving types are: inside (2,1,1) (162 candidates), one outsider plus old triple (2,1,0) (108 support triples), and inside (3,1,0) (36 candidates). These are 306 normalized support patterns. No fourth type first becomes admissible at ten.
  1. Minimal deletion-cover templates. For each surviving representative C, let H_C join old sets F_i,F_j when F_i,F_j,C form a sunflower. Any deletion set permitting C is a vertex cover of H_C. A complete branch-on-uncovered-edge enumerator, safely pruned by a disjoint-edge matching, finds every inclusion-minimal cover of size at most ten. There are 44 templates across the three representatives: 32 of size 6 for the first type; 2 of size 5 and 2 of size 7 for the outsider type; and 8 of size 9 for the third type. Every ten-cover contains one of these templates, since repeatedly deleting redundant cover vertices produces a minimal cover of size at most ten. This argument makes the superset scan complete without assuming all ten-covers are minimal.
  1. Every ten-superset of a template. The separate optimized C++ scanner enumerates all size-ten supersets of those 44 templates, with multiplicity:
   32*C(48,4) + 2*C(49,5) + 2*C(47,3) + 8*C(45,1)
   = 10,073,118 visits.

Each visit tests all 306 candidate forbidden-pair graphs. An inside candidate contributes at most one insertion. An outside triple T contributes at most one if an old superset of T survives, and at most two otherwise; three completions of T form a sunflower. This ignores cross-candidate clashes and hence is a safe upper bound. The scanner finds 18,730 visits of individual capacity at least five, comprising 9,841 distinct ten-deletion masks relative to the fixed representatives. The latter is not a count of all globally labeled ten-deletion patterns.

  1. Independent literal replay of the critical masks. Python reconstructs the forbidden graphs and every surviving old set using frozenset intersections, independently verifies every individually allowed candidate's old-old-new compatibility, and recomputes the exact capacity distribution:

Only the 3,005 masks of capacity at least six can permit six simultaneous insertions. For each, enumerate every six-slot subset (6,209 in total), and every equality partition of the outsider labels, rejecting duplicate sets (35,971 admissible partition instances). In every case, a new-new-old or new-new-new sunflower occurs. All old-old-new triples were checked separately; the original old-old-old triples are sunflower-free. Hence no six insertions are possible. Any larger insertion family contains a six-subfamily, and fewer than ten deletions can be enlarged to ten. The explicit witness proves the bound of five is sharp.

The exact scanner's multiplicity count is checked against the binomial identity above. Its high-capacity output is deduplicated and independently recomputed in Python; the literal all-triples witness check is also replayed with the existing integer-mask sunflower engine. This is a finite exhaustive proof candidate, not a solver verdict or independent specialist review.

The test suite challenges the minimal cover filter, 11-disjoint-pair exclusions, outsider partitions, essentiality of each deletion and a forged sixth insertion. No random seed or stochastic search enters the final certificate.

Failed avenues, adversarial corrections and next falsifier#

  • False first lead: an integer program restricted to one nine-point component appeared to permit five inside insertions after nine deletions. Its model omitted empty-core sunflowers involving an old set in the other component. An independent literal replay exposed five retained old sets disjoint from proposed new sets; the corrected model was infeasible. The apparent nine-deletion counterexample was invalid and is not evidence against the previous theorem.
  • Eight-deletion witness extension: retaining the four insertions of the earlier sharp eight-deletion example, adding at most two deletions and one further four-set yielded no valid five-insertion witness in an exact 22-point candidate sweep. This is a local negative, not the general ten-deletion upper bound.
  • Nine-deletion high-capacity extensions: extending the 131 nine-cover masks of individual capacity at least five by one deletion generated 5,641 distinct ten-masks; 24,846 five-slot selections and 69,906 outsider-label partitions gave no witness. This heuristic restriction was incomplete. The full minimal cover scan found the five-insertion witness outside that subset.
  • Stronger extrapolation falsified: the nine-deletion bound of four insertions does not extend to ten deletions. The explicit five-insertion witness refutes it.
