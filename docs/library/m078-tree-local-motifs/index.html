M078: local-ball obstruction to compressed tree-card reconstruction

2026-11-02. Claim #267, X-M078-TREE-LOCAL-MOTIF, SHA-256 7642e875424b6b4d3a433be1030a9dca00da1b6b7403b3dc16841a741a22b91a. Author-checked restricted negative result; no independent specialist review or historical-novelty assessment. Not a counterexample to graph reconstruction: trees are reconstructible from the full vertex-deleted deck.

Let i_F(X) count vertex subsets inducing unlabeled graph F. Define M_k(G) as the multiset over vertices v of vectors (i_F(G-v): |V(F)|<=k) over all unlabeled motifs, including disconnected ones. The vector for each card stays paired. Write S_deg(G) for the multiset of sorted degree sequences of the cards.

General lemma. If two graphs have the same multiset of rooted radius-(k-1) induced balls, their M_k are equal. Proof: for connected F of order t<=k, the number a_F(G,v) of induced copies containing v is determined by that rooted ball. Since t*i_F(G)=sum_v a_F(G,v), the global connected counts and paired card counts i_F(G-v)=i_F(G)-a_F(G,v) match. For disconnected F=C_1 disjoint-union ... disjoint-union C_c, the product prod_i i_{C_i}(X) equals a positive multiple of i_F(X) plus an integer linear combination of induced counts of graphs of order at most |F| with fewer connected components: overlapping selected copies or cross-edges reduce the number of components. Induct on c to recover every disconnected count from connected counts, separately for each card. No assumption that the graphs are trees is needed.

All-orders theorem. For each k>=3, n>=6k-6, set L=2k-3, s=n-(6k-6). The nonisomorphic subcubic trees S(L,L,L+2+s) and S(L,L+1,L+1+s) have identical M_k and S_deg, where S(a,b,c) is the tree with one degree-three center and three pendant paths of edge lengths a,b,c. The unordered leg-length triples differ. Put r=k-1. Every leg has length at least 2r-1, so no vertex lies both within distance r-1 of the center and distance r-1 of a leaf. Rooted radius-r balls split into center-near types (center and r-1 vertices per leg), leaf-near types (r per leg), and middle rooted paths (remaining sum(leg lengths)-3(2r-1) vertices). Each type and multiplicity depends only on r, the number of legs and their total length, not their individual lengths. At a radius boundary a branch or leaf is truncated and its special degree is invisible. Apply the lemma. As r>=2, the rooted balls also determine each root's degree and neighbor degrees; together with the global degree multiset, these determine the card degree sequence. Thus S_deg agrees.

Sharp small-degree boundary. The first nonisomorphic trees with equal S_deg have 10 vertices, witnessed by S(1,3,5) and S(1,4,4). More generally S(1,3,n-5) and S(1,4,n-6) work for every n>=10: both have the same degree-neighbor profile multiset (one degree-three vertex adjacent to degrees 1,2,2; one leaf adjacent to degree three; two leaves adjacent to degree two; matching degree-two neighbor types), and a vertex's card degree sequence depends only on this profile and the global degrees. The minimality assertion is a finite exact census: a separate C++17 program enumerates all Prüfer sequences for 3<=n<=9, independently canonicalizes whole trees by AHU, and finds no collision among respectively 1,2,3,6,11,23,47 isomorphism types. Orders 1 and 2 each have one tree.

Reproduce: python3 scripts/m078_tree_local_motifs.py --check; python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_m078_tree_local_motifs.py' -v; g++ -std=c++17 -O3 -o /tmp/m078_tree_census scripts/m078_tree_degree_census.cpp && /tmp/m078_tree_census. Python directly enumerates and canonically relabels every induced motif up to k=4 in every card, independently of its rooted-ball check: (k,n)=(2,12),(3,12),(4,18) and one-vertex extensions for k=3,4 pass. Seven adversarial tests pass. An optional third path, python3 scripts/m078_tree_networkx_crosscheck.py (NetworkX 3.6.1), uses the graph atlas and exact graph-isomorphism comparisons rather than bitmask canonicalization; it independently reproduced the k=4,n=18 paired motif vectors in two ~6-second passes. It is a separate author-controlled implementation, not independent specialist review. The C++ labeled-Prüfer census examines 3,16,125,1296,16807,262144,4782969 labeled trees for orders 3 through 9, with zero card-degree collisions. A separate exploratory NetworkX unlabeled-tree enumeration also first collides at order 10. These are author-controlled code paths, not independent mathematical acceptance.

Prior art and scope: Kelly (1957), DOI 10.2140/pjm.1957.7.961, proves reconstruction of trees from full cards. Groenland–Johnston–Scott–Tan (2025), DOI 10.1007/s11856-025-2858-3, treats induced decks of much larger card order. Spinoza–West, arXiv:1609.00284, develops bounded k-deck collisions in maximum-degree-two graphs. Bondy–Hemminger (1977), DOI 10.1002/jgt.3190010306, surveys reconstruction. Existing repo M078 unicyclic obstruction, #262, concerns a different graph class/statistic. Similar local indistinguishability ideas are known; historical novelty is not established.

Failed stronger avenues: The shorter S(k,k,k+2) / S(k,k+1,k+1) template is distinguished by paired motif counts at k=4 and k=5. Shortening L=2k-3 to 2k-4 fails for tested k=3,4,5. The sufficient 6k-6 order bound is not claimed optimal among all trees. Next falsifiers: independently audit the disconnected-motif induction and radius-boundary classification; independently reimplement unlabeled induced-subgraph counting for k>=5; seek smaller M_k collisions and test augmentations by card diameter/component-size vectors; audit historical priority.
