November 2026 RH / Dirichlet GRH: verified scope and research impact

Status as of 2026-11-21: external formal certification reported; not reproduced or independently accepted by Project 100. This is a research intake and dependency audit, not a new proof, solution ledger update, or cryptanalytic breakthrough.

Primary evidence and exact scope#

  1. Lean/Mathlib joint statement (2026-11-21) certifies the ordinary Riemann Hypothesis theorem in Mathlib and reports independent proof export and kernel checks of Alpöge's 2026-11-20 Lean library.
  2. Andrew Sutherland's MIT verification record reports compilation of 175,187 modules, axiom audits, and independent checks. Its five exported theorems include ordinary RH and GRH for Dirichlet L-functions; the record explicitly says “GRH” here means Dirichlet L-functions only.
  3. Contemporary mathematical outline is an exposition, not an independent formal verification or proof of additional L-function cases.

Do not conflate: ordinary RH (Riemann zeta), Dirichlet GRH (all Dirichlet character L-functions), and broader Dedekind/Hecke/Artin/automorphic GRH. No source above certifies the latter families. A formal theorem establishes its encoded statement relative to its trusted foundations; external checks do not constitute Project 100's own replay, semantic review, or independent acceptance. Clay/prize adjudication and wider mathematical assimilation are separate matters.

Newly usable arithmetic transfer#

The MIT verification also reports an independent proof composition with the Robin1984 Lean formalization (commit acab1a3), yielding unconditional, kernel-checked statements of Robin's inequality for all integers above 5040 and Lagarias's divisor-sum inequality. This is a stronger practical exemplar for Project 100 than simply recording a headline: it demonstrates a pathway from a newly checked theorem to independent downstream arithmetic definitions. See the MIT arithmetic consequences section. Project 100 has not reconstructed that bridge.

Machine-readable dependency deliverable#

The source-of-truth 18-case dependency audit separates five eligible for precise corollary audit, seven requiring exact theorem matching, and six blocked by stronger-family assumptions. “Eligible” means worth verifying against the original mathematical literature, not mathematically accepted by Project 100. Active targets M001/M002 are mapped to the external theorem names and remain pending independent scope audit.

The validator rejects treating Dedekind/Hecke/Artin/automorphic cases as certified by the Dirichlet proof and checks the active registry’s question wording so a future scope change is caught. Negative-control tests cover false local replay attestations, target substitution, invalid provenance URLs, and dependency promotion. It runs in the repository's portfolio CI:

python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_grh_dependency_audit.py' -v
