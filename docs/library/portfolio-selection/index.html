Top 100 portfolio selection and admission

First screening: October 31, 2026. The active 100, scores and proof statuses remain unchanged.

The screening ledger flags 15 entries and names five alternatives. Flags are editorial hypotheses, not conclusions: unflagged does not mean verified, and a candidate source link does not mean an independent current open-status audit is complete. Check the ledger with python3 scripts/portfolio_triage.py --check.

Keep three decisions separate#

  1. Membership asks if a well-defined open problem is sufficiently consequential and distinctive to deserve one of 100 scarce slots. Prestige is relevant, but breadth of mathematical impact, distinctness, and method transfer matter. Do not automatically exclude hard problems.
  2. Execution asks which targets can support reproducible useful work now. The existing execution queue correctly rewards working campaigns and independent exact checkers.
  3. Evidence asks whether the precise statement remains open. Compare the newest primary specialist literature against claimed resolutions. Preprint postings, formal-statement compilers and independent mathematical acceptance are different levels of evidence.

The current weighted ranking devotes 30% to attackability. It is an editorial work-priority heuristic, not an authoritative ordering of the 100 greatest unsolved theorems. Do not silently rewrite scores.

Review outcomes and gates#

audit_claimAn external manuscript may resolve the targetExact theorem-scope match, independent proof/dependency and specialist reviews

A candidate is not admission-ready until it has: (1) an exact falsifiable statement; (2) a dated contemporary primary specialist source establishing the still-open remainder, plus a search for later claims; (3) a distinctness and implication map against the active 100; (4) a realistic first lemma or certificate with explicit verification cost; and (5) a dated, source-backed replacement comparison in a PR.

Never replace a problem solely because it is difficult or because an unreviewed paper claims a proof. A full accepted resolution requires the stronger Solved archive rules. A duplicate, underspecified target or deprioritized target is not a solved problem. Stable IDs and prior campaign history must be preserved.

Initial statement-scope dossiers (2026-10-31)#

The five source-annotated statement drafts cover M017, M053, M056, M063, and M070. They record exact modeling choices still to settle, example formulations, and primary/background links. All are marked background_only: inspecting these sources does not verify that the example target is the final canonical formulation or still open.

A concrete distinction: planar exponential first-passage fluctuations (M063) are not interchangeable with solvable exponential last-passage models. The proposed M063 benchmark asks whether variance is n^(2/3+o(1)) for passage time from (0,0) to (n,0) with i.i.d. rate-one exponential nearest-neighbor edge weights. It is a formulation for independent specialist review, not an accepted theorem or registry change.

M017 requires selection of a still-open functorial transfer after modern symmetric-power results. M053 requires explicit genericity and data topology. M056 must separate 3D diagonal, bounded-data and higher-dimensional sharp exponent regimes. M070 requires specifying fields, rescaling and convergence.
