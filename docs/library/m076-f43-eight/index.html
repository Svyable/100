RESEARCH DOSSIERS

M076 — sharp eight-deletion stability of the classical 54-set family

Date: 2026-11-02 UTC. Claim: #212. Work ID: X-M076-F43-EIGHT. Scope SHA-256: b22d7b8a1bc3115b51cc948c3068fca1c255d6622f0edcc05e58eb4077688a9e.

Status: Author-derived exact restricted theorem candidate, supported by two algorithmically distinct exhaustive cover enumerators, a separate literal set-intersection/cover-existence sweep, and a sharp explicit witness. No independent mathematical review or historical priority audit. This is not a global bound on the sunflower function or a resolution of the Erdős–Rado conjecture.

Statement#

Let F be the following explicitly defined 54-member family on U={0,...,17}: partition U into two nine-point components, each partitioned into three blocks of size three; in each component take every four-set with two points from each of two different blocks. There are 2 × 3 × 3 × 3 = 54 such sets. A three-sunflower is three distinct sets with identical pairwise intersections, including the empty intersection.

Eight-deletion theorem candidate (sharp): For every ambient set V ⊇ U, every D ⊆ F with |D| ≤ 8, and every collection I ⊆ binom(V,4) \\ F of distinct new sets, if (F \\ D) ∪ I is three-sunflower-free, then |I| ≤ 4. The bound is attained with eight deletions:

  • Delete the old sets at base_family() indices {0,1,10,11,18,21,25,26}.
  • Insert {0,1,3,6}, {0,1,3,7}, {0,1,4,8}, {0,1,5,8}.

The resulting 50-set family is checked against all 19,600 triples, with zero sunflowers. The stronger extension of the seven-deletion |I|≤2 claim to eight deletions is false. The new upper bound does not yield a 55-set construction: eight deletions would require nine insertions to exceed 54.

Exact certificate and completeness proof#

  1. All ambient universes reduce to 18 candidate orbits. Permute elements within each three-point block, blocks within components, the two components, and all outside points. A new four-set is characterized by the number of outsiders r∈{0,...,4} and the unordered pair of sorted block-occupancy triples. All 18 types are realized on the 22-point set U∪{18,19,20,21} (8, 5, 3, 1, 1 types for r=0,1,2,3,4), comprising 7,261 nonmembers. The arbitrary number of outsider labels cannot introduce a new type; their multiplicity is handled below.
  1. Nine-disjoint-pair obstruction for 16 types. For a candidate C, make a graph on the 54 old sets, joining i,j when F_i,F_j,C form a sunflower. Any deletion set allowing C is a vertex cover of this graph. The source file contains nine vertex-disjoint verified edges for each of 16 candidate orbit representatives, excluding those types under eight deletions. The only survivors are the inside type (0,(2,1,1),(0,0,0)) and one-outsider type (1,(2,1,0),(0,0,0)). Their candidates are exactly 162 inside four-sets and 108 old-support triples, the latter completed by an arbitrary single outside point.
  1. Every eight-cover, two enumerations. For the inside and outside representatives, greedy disjoint matchings have respectively six and five edges. Choosing an endpoint from each matched edge and respectively two or three further old vertices, then testing all forbidden edges, gives 32,416 and 35,811 eight-covers. A different recursion branches on an uncovered forbidden edge, prunes by a disjoint-matching lower bound, and expands all discovered covers to size eight. It agrees as a set on both counts. There are 16 covers common to the two representatives, hence 68,211 distinct eight-cover patterns relative to the fixed orbit representatives (not a count of globally distinct labeled deletion sets).
  1. Simultaneous insertion upper bound. For each of those 68,211 covers, test all 270 admissible old-support patterns against their full forbidden-pair graphs. Each inside candidate contributes at most one new set. For each outside triple T, if an old set containing T survives, at most one T∪{x} completion can be inserted (two completions plus that old set form a sunflower). Otherwise at most two completions can be inserted (three completions form a sunflower). Sum these individual upper bounds, deliberately ignoring any conflicts between distinct candidates. Every row has upper bound at most four. Combined with the explicit 50-set sharpness witness, this proves the claimed local optimum conditional on the correctness of the finite certificate. For |D|<8, extend D to eight old sets: deleting more old sets can only relax insertion constraints.

  1. Separate literal adversary. An orbit-free frozenset implementation checks each of the 7,261 nonmember four-sets on 22 points, rebuilding forbidden pairs from literal intersections. A recursive cover-existence test, with an independently computed greedy matching lower bound, retains exactly 594 labeled candidates (162 inside, 432 outside completions). Normalizing outsider labels gives precisely the 270 patterns above. The sharp witness is also checked by independently represented literal sets against all triples. These are separate computational paths by the same author, not independent specialist proof acceptance.

Ten dedicated tests exercise exact cover agreement, forbidden-pair witness validity, a forged eight-deletion cover, a malformed repeated vertex pair, outside orbit exclusion, the independent 22-point sweep, the sharp four-insertion witness, rejection of a fifth candidate, and all 19,600 triples of the final 50-set family. No SAT/ILP verdict is used in the proof certificate; SciPy and NetworkX were used only for exploration of minimum covers/matchings, and their outputs were reduced to literal verified matching witnesses.

Exploratory failures: Extrapolating the seven-deletion two-insertion upper bound fails: four insertions are possible after eight deletions. An eight-edge matching is not sufficient to exclude the (3,1,0) inside type; it has a nine-edge witness, which is essential at this threshold. Counting admissible patterns alone would overestimate multiplicities of outside completions; the surviving-old-superset rule is needed. The local eight-deletion maximum does not improve the global lower bound.

Next falsifiers: A candidate type missing from the 18 orbits; an invalid nine-edge witness; an eight-cover omitted by either enumerator; a cover allowing a fifth unit of insertion capacity; a new set with two outsiders surviving the literal sweep; a sunflower in the 50-set sharp witness; or a prior published theorem already implying the same eight-deletion bound. The nine-deletion neighborhood and general 55-set search remain untouched. Independent combinatorics and historical novelty review are necessary before any acceptance claim.
