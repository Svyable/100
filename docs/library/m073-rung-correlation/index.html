RESEARCH DOSSIERS

M073 — a seven-vertex rung improves the two-block stability certificate

2026-11-01 · X-M073-RUNG-CORRELATION · claim #167.

Status: Author-derived restricted inequality, not independently reviewed or established novel. This is a stronger sufficient condition on an equal-mass, off-diagonal-dominant two-block graphon for the source-pinned 35-vertex, 66-edge incidence graph H. It neither resolves Sidorenko's conjecture nor constructs the finite host alleged in the September 2026 preprint. The Ising correlation monotonicity input is classical, not our theorem.

The new local motif#

Let F be a simple 3-uniform hypergraph with the following local pair multiplicity property: whenever a pair {x,y} occurs in a triple, it occurs in exactly two distinct triples. Let H(F) be its point–triple incidence graph.

Rung lemma. Every incidence edge i–f, with f={i,j,k}, lies in a seven-vertex, nine-edge subgraph consisting of

  • the edge i–f;
  • the two internally disjoint three-edge paths i–g_ij–j–f and i–g_ik–k–f; and
  • a two-edge rung j–g_jk–k.

Here g_xy is the unique triple other than f containing {x,y}. The three auxiliary triples are pairwise distinct: if, for example, g_ij=g_ik, that triple contains i,j,k and therefore equals f, contradicting the definition of g_ij. The same argument applies to the other pairs. All seven vertices are distinct and all nine edges are present. This proves the lemma.

The source-pinned M073 table has 22 triples on 13 points, 33 occurring point-pairs, and every occurring pair occurs exactly twice. Consequently the lemma applies to all 66 incidence edges. The checker constructs and validates all 66 actual subgraphs; it does not infer their existence merely from the number of four-cycles.

Exact ferromagnetic endpoint correlation#

For a finite graph G, give independent spins tau_v in {-1,+1} the zero-field ferromagnetic weight

product_{uv in E(G)} (1 + qtau_utau_v), 0 <= q < 1.

The usual high-temperature expansion says that the denominator of the two-point correlation at vertices i,f is the generating polynomial of even-degree edge subsets, while the numerator is the polynomial of edge subsets with odd-degree boundary {i,f}. For the rung motif, enumerating its 2^9 edge subsets (or a short parity argument) gives

D(q) = 1 + 3q^4 + 4q^6, N(q) = q + 2q^3 + 3q^5 + 2*q^7.

Therefore its exact endpoint correlation is

K(q) = N(q)/D(q) = q(1 + q^2 + 2q^4)/(1 - q^2 + 4*q^4). (1)

The prior two-bypass subgraph (without the rung) has correlation

K0(q) = (q + 2q^3 + q^7)/(1 + 2q^4 + q^6).

Clearing denominators, the improvement is the polynomial identity

N(q)(1+2q^4+q^6) - (q+2q^3+q^7)D(q) = 2q^5(1-q^2)^3*(1+q^2). (2)

All denominators are positive for 0<=q<1. Thus K(q)>K0(q) for every 0<q<1, with equality at q=0 and in the q→1 limit.

For the full H(F), the classical Griffiths–Kelly–Sherman second correlation inequality (GKS-II) implies that adding the remaining nonnegative ferromagnetic couplings cannot decrease the correlation of the fixed endpoints. Hence every incidence edge of the pinned H has ferromagnetic correlation at least K(q).

This is a structural improvement over the previously merged two-bypass estimate, not a new ferromagnetic correlation principle.

Stronger M073 inequality#

Consider an equal-mass symmetric two-block graphon with matrix

W = [[u+delta, b], [b, u-delta]],

where 0<u<=b<=1, 0<=delta<=min(u,1-u). Put

p=(u+b)/2, q=(b-u)/(b+u), h=delta/u.

The previously established spin-flip pairing, AM–GM and Jensen argument gives for every bipartite F with m edges

t(F,W)/t(F,W0) >= (1-h^2)^[m*(1-C)/4],

provided C is a common lower bound on the ferromagnetic edge correlations of F after flipping spins on one bipartition, and W0=[[u,b],[b,u]]. This follows because the expected number of monochromatic edges is at most m*(1-C)/2.

For the pinned 66-edge H, use C=K(q) from (1). The earlier even-subgraph bound t(H,W0)>=p^66(1+33q^4+88*q^6) then proves the continuous-parameter inequality

t(H,W) >= p^66 * (1+33q^4+88q^6) * (1-h^2)^[66*(1-K(q))/4]. (3)

For h=1 interpret the right side by continuity (it is zero). The inequality holds for all real weights in the stated domain. Its right side is a strict improvement over the previous two-bypass bound when 0<q<1 and 0<h<1; it is not always at least p^66. The domain is only a restricted two-block graphon family.

An exact rational host the old certificate cannot certify#

Choose

(a,b,c) = (241/400, 1, 159/400). u=1/2, delta=41/400, p=3/4, q=1/3, h=41/200.

At q=1/3 the two-bypass bound has K0=223/561 and exponent 66(1-K0)/4=169/17. The rung has K=23/57 and exponent 66(1-K)/4=187/19. The common cycle factor is

1+33(1/3)^4+88(1/3)^6 = 1114/729.

Write R=1-h^2=38319/40000. Exact rational comparisons give

(1114/729)^17 * R^169 < 1, (4) (1114/729)^19 * R^187 > 1. (5)

Thus the old source-specific sufficient condition does not certify Sidorenko for this host, but the new rung bound does. The new certified ratio exceeds 1.0014828. The exact density ratio is approximately 3.224523, independently computed by three algorithms (point-label sum, histogram compression, and pairwise factor elimination). The actual density is much larger than either bound; the gain is in the rigorously certified region, not a claimed near-counterexample.

Reproduction and adversarial falsifiers#

Run from the repository root:

python3 scripts/m073_rung_correlation.py --check python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_m073_rung_correlation.py' -v python3 scripts/regen.py --check

The source is loaded with the existing SHA-256 guard 653fd38252aa4034512303dd71d738a42cc3685666b982d41f21c76c336c4b02 on the canonical 22-triple array. The checker enumerates all 66 motifs, all 512 rung edge subsets, and 128 independent spin assignments for each of five rational couplings. It independently recomputes the two correlation polynomials and the exact threshold comparisons; it also compares three independent source-density algorithms on the rational example.

Adversarial controls: mutating a source triple destroys the required pair multiplicity; deleting one of the two rung edges makes the extra vertex a leaf and recovers exactly the old two-bypass correlation; negative or out-of-range coupling values fail closed. The 66-edge source audit, not a sampled graphon grid, is the finite premise of the continuum proof. A code pass is not an independent mathematical review.

Failed avenue: A third internally vertex-disjoint bypass from i to f cannot exist in this motif: f has degree three, and its two neighbors other than i are already used by the two length-three bypasses. Adding the rung instead of a third bypass produces the strict improvement (2).
