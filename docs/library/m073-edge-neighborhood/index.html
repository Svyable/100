RESEARCH DOSSIERS

M073 — two-square edge neighborhoods sharpen the source-pinned Ising bound

November 2, 2026 · work ID X-M073-EDGE-NEIGHBORHOOD · claim #272. Evidence level: author-derived restricted continuum inequality and finite exact source certificate; no independent specialist proof review and no historical novelty determination. This is not a construction of the external finite Sidorenko host and does not resolve, affirm or refute the universal conjecture.

Precisely bounded result#

Let H be the 35-vertex, 66-edge point–face incidence graph of the 22 triples in the pinned source table. For an equal-mass symmetric two-block graphon with matrix

           [ u + delta       b ]
       W = [                   ],
           [     b       u - delta ]

assume 0 < u <= b <= 1, 0 <= delta <= u, and u + delta <= 1. Put

p=(u+b)/2, q=(b-u)/(b+u), h=delta/u, R=1-h^2.

Define three polynomials (variable q):

D  = 1 + 5q^4 + 6q^6 + 11q^8 + 6q^10 + 3q^12,
NA = q + 2q^3 + 7q^5 + 6q^7 + 11q^9 + 4q^11 + q^13,
NB = q + 2q^3 + 6q^5 + 9q^7 +  8q^9 + 5q^11 + q^13.
KA=NA/D, KB=NB/D.

Let Z_H(q)=sum_{k=0}^{22} c_k q^(2k) be the complete even-subgraph polynomial already proved by two finite recurrences in M073_INFORMATION_ALIGNMENT.md. The coefficients c_k are the 23 entries in data/experiments/m073_information_alignment.json; in particular c_0=1, c_2=33, c_3=88, and all are nonnegative.

Restricted theorem candidate (with a source-table finite premise). For all real parameters in the domain above,

t(H,W) >= p^66 * Z_H(q) * R^E(q),
E(q) = (66 - 54 KA(q) - 12 KB(q))/4.                       (1)

At h=1 the right side is interpreted as zero. For 0<q<1, 0<h<1, this is a strict improvement over the previous seven-vertex rung/Jensen/GKS certificate even after that older certificate is strengthened by the full even-subgraph factor Z_H(q), rather than only its 4- and 6-cycle truncation. The old exponent is 66(1-K0(q))/4 where

K0(q) = (q + 2q^3 + 3q^5 + 2q^7)/(1+3q^4+4q^6).

Formula (1) is confined to the stated two-block family and the source-pinned H; the structural mechanism extends to the surface incidence class in (1s) below. The exact host claimed in the September 2026 manuscript is not checked here.

Structural link-cycle lemma (not limited to the pinned table)#

The two-square mechanism has a general geometric formulation. Let T be any finite closed simplicial triangulated surface, possibly disconnected, whose every vertex has degree at least four. Let H(T) be its point–triangle incidence graph, let m=3|F(T)|, and let v4 be the number of degree-four vertices of T. Link-cycle lemma: every point–face incidence edge of H(T) contains the same 11-vertex 15-edge A- or B-type two-square subgraph, and its type is B if and only if its point vertex has degree four. Hence exactly 4*v4 rooted incidences have type B and m-4*v4 have type A.

Proof. The link of a vertex i is a simple cycle of length d(i)>=4. The face {i,j,k} corresponds to the link edge jk. The two other faces on ij and ik correspond to the preceding and succeeding edges of that cycle. The third points of these faces are distinct because the link is not a triangle. Their further completions h_ij and h_ik coincide precisely when the preceding and succeeding two-step link edges coincide, which occurs exactly for a four-cycle, d(i)=4. If they coincide, use instead the square along j-g_jk; its completion face cannot contain i, since ij already belongs to the two distinct faces f and g_ij. Thus it is distinct from h_ij. All the required new vertices are distinct: a third-point collision would make the link of one of i,j,k a triangle, forbidden by the degree-four minimum. Every specified edge belongs to an actual point–face incidence, so the 11-vertex 15-edge subgraph exists. There are exactly d(i) incidence roots at each point i, giving the count. □

The same GKS/Jensen proof consequently gives the general surface incidence bound

t(H(T),W) >= p^m Z_H(T)(q) *
 R^[(m - (m-4*v4)*KA(q) - 4*v4*KB(q))/4].                (1s)

Here Z_H(T)(q) denotes the even-subgraph generating polynomial of H(T); no particular surface topology, genus or source-table spectrum is assumed. This is a restricted two-block statement, not a general Sidorenko theorem. The pinned 22-triangle system is checked to have connected cycle links with degree histogram {4:3,5:6,6:4}; hence v4=3, m=66, and the general lemma explains the 54/12 classification without depending on label ordering. An independent link-graph checker confirms this premise, and the test suite challenges the lemma on the octahedral sphere (all 24 roots B) and icosahedral sphere (all 60 roots A), as well as a boundary and a degree-three negative control.

Local graph construction and finite source premise#

Fix a point–face incidence edge i-f, with f={i,j,k} and j<k. Each occurring point pair lies in exactly two source triples. Write g_ij,g_ik,g_jk for the other faces containing the respective pairs. These four faces and the three points i,j,k form the seven-vertex, nine-edge rung graph of M073_RUNG_CORRELATION.md.

Let x_ij,x_ik,x_jk be the third points of the three alternate faces (outside f). The three points are distinct for the pinned table. Let h_ij be the other face containing {i,x_ij}, h_ik the other face containing {i,x_ik}, and h_jk the other face containing {j,x_jk}.

A square graft along i-g_ij adds the three-edge path i-h_ij-x_ij-g_ij, making a four-cycle. Graft a second square along i-g_ik if h_ij != h_ik; otherwise graft along j-g_jk. The source table has the following complete finite classification:

TypeConditionRooted incidencesGrafted rung edges
Ah_ij != h_ik54i-g_ij, i-g_ik
Bh_ij = h_ik12i-g_ij, j-g_jk

For all 66 roots, h_ij != h_jk. Thus the two selected new points and two new faces are distinct, and the construction contains 11 vertices and 15 specified edges. Four source neighborhoods have one additional incidence edge: the argument deliberately uses only the specified 15-edge subgraph, not an unjustified claim that every induced neighborhood has 15 edges.

This finite premise is checked in two ways: an explicit pair-to-faces map and a separate incidence-neighbor search. Both enumerate all 66 roots and confirm the 54/12 split; both reject an adversarially mutated source triple. The SHA-256 guard on the 22-triple table is inherited from m073_assortative2.load_faces. The 54/12 split is not asserted for arbitrary pair-twice triple systems.

Exact local correlations and strict gain#

For a finite graph G, assign uniform spins tau_v in {-1,+1} and weight each edge by 1+q*tau_v*tau_w. The high-temperature expansion gives

E_G[tau_i tau_f] =
  (sum_{S subset E(G), boundary(S)={i,f}} q^|S|)
  / (sum_{S subset E(G), boundary(S)=empty} q^|S|).

Direct enumeration of all 2^15=32768 edge subsets of each abstract two-square graph yields the common denominator D and the numerators NA and NB above. A separate direct sum over all 2^11=2048 spin assignments agrees exactly for rational values q=0,1/3,3/4 (and additional test values). This is algorithmic cross-verification, not outside mathematical review.

The strict polynomial identities are

KA-KB = q^5 (1-q^2)^3 / D,                              (2)

KB-K0 = q^5 (1-q^2)^3 (1+q^2)
        * (1+3q^2+2q^4+2q^6) / (D * D0),               (3)

D0 = 1+3q^4+4q^6.

Every denominator is positive on [0,1), and every numerator in (2)–(3) is strictly positive for 0<q<1. Consequently the two-square correlations are uniformly better than the rung bound, and the 54 A-type roots have a further strict gain.

To pass from local to full H, give each graph edge ferromagnetic coupling atanh(q)>=0. The classical Griffiths–Kelly–Sherman second inequality implies that adding all remaining edges of H cannot decrease E[tau_i tau_f]. Thus each full-graph incidence correlation is at least KA on the 54 A roots and KB on the 12 B roots.

For completeness, the earlier AM–GM/Jensen spin-flip proof pairs each spin assignment with its global reversal, obtaining

t(H,W)/t(H,W0) >= R^( E_mu0[M] / 2 ),
W0=[[u,b],[b,u]],

where M is the number of edges monochromatic in the original spin labels. Flipping one bipartition turns the baseline into a ferromagnet; for each edge e, Pr(e monochromatic)=(1-E[tau_i tau_f])/2. The edgewise bounds give

E_mu0[M] <= (66 - 54 KA - 12 KB)/2.

Because 0<R<=1, the Jensen inequality yields t(H,W)/t(H,W0) >= R^E(q). Finally the exact high-temperature identity t(H,W0)=p^66 Z_H(q) proves (1). The case h=1 follows by continuity.

Exact rational boundary witness#

Choose

(a,b,c)=(12443/20000, 1, 7557/20000),
u=1/2, p=3/4, q=1/3, h=2443/10000,
R=94031751/100000000.

At q=1/3 the exact source spectrum and local correlations give

Z_H(1/3) = 598495823780642816000 / 328256967394537077627,
KA(1/3) = 21899/53397,
KB(1/3) = 21755/53397,
E_new = 10199/1047,
E_uniform_B = 174031/17799,
E_rung = 187/19.

The checker proves all three strict inequalities by rational integer powers, without floating-point tolerance:

Z_H(1/3)^19    * R^187    < 1,     (rung + full spectrum)
Z_H(1/3)^17799 * R^174031 < 1,     (uniform B + full spectrum)
Z_H(1/3)^1047  * R^10199  > 1.     (54/12 weighted bound) (4)

The corresponding rung/uniform-B/weighted lower ratios are approximately 0.994975110, 0.998934253 and 1.001174748. This witness is certified by (1) but not even by the uniform two-square B inequality with the complete 23-coefficient spectrum. The 54/12 source-specific weighting is necessary for this particular certificate. The exact actual density ratio, computed by direct 8192-point-label summation and independently by the source-profile histogram, is approximately 4.061229761. The result is a stronger sufficient certificate, not a near-violation or a counterexample to Sidorenko.

Reproduction, adversaria and failure log#

From repository root:

python3 scripts/m073_edge_neighborhood.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_m073_edge_neighborhood.py' -v
python3 scripts/regen.py --check --fast
python3 scripts/regen.py --check
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests

The checker rederives the 54/12 source classification by two independently written searches, enumerates parity polynomials from edge subsets, checks them against direct spin sums, verifies the strict factorization identities and uses both the source spin and parity recurrences for Z_H. The rational witness uses exact large-integer inequalities. Tests corrupt a source triple, introduce a repeated point, break a square edge, test the q domain and challenge both strict-gain identities.

Failed or corrected avenues:

  • Adding only the three third points of the alternate faces makes dangling leaves and does not improve endpoint correlation.
  • A uniform choice of the two squares attached at i fails at 12 roots because their completion faces coincide. The B-type alternative restores a distinct two-square motif.
  • Assuming every resulting induced 11-vertex neighborhood has exactly 15 edges is false at four roots; the proof uses a 15-edge subgraph, with monotonicity handling extras.
  • The first rational separation used only 1+33q^4+88q^6, already dominated by the repository's full even-subgraph polynomial. The final witness (4) challenges the stronger full-spectrum rung certificate instead.
  • A three-square 13-vertex uniform extension is obstructed by repeated completion faces at 12 roots; no three-square bound is asserted. Nothing here certifies all off-diagonal-dominant two-block parameters.

Next falsifiers: independent external proof audit of the 54/12 source classification and the Jensen/GKS transfer; independently reimplement the two-square spin polynomials without this code; seek a larger uniformly embeddable motif or an actual two-block negative Sidorenko gap outside (1)'s sufficient tube. The full conjecture, the 2026 claimed finite host, and historical novelty remain separate questions.
