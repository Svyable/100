METHOD & GOVERNANCE

Contributing to 100

The goal is not to collect plausible conjectures or dramatic claims. It is to maintain 100 exact, high-value proof/counterexample targets and produce independently checkable advances.

The Next 100 hypothesis reservoir accepts unvetted, cross-domain\nideas separately from the active 100. A discovery score alone cannot promote an idea.\n\n## Ways to contribute

  1. Status correction — supply the precise theorem statement, a dated primary source, and explain whether it proves or refutes the exact full conjecture.
  2. New lemma — state all hypotheses and dependencies, give a complete proof or a trusted finite certificate, and identify the remaining unrestricted gap.
  3. Executable evidence — supply exact inputs, the verifier, expected outputs, resource usage, and a reproducing command. No unverifiable solver verdicts.
  4. New candidate — first propose an immutable N-ID hypothesis in the reservoir, with cross-domain consequences and a first falsifiable attack; propose replacing a resolved, duplicated or weak candidate; preserve stable IDs and history. Do not silently exceed 100.
  5. Shared attack shape — extend data/methods.json when several problems should move together. A shape is a template, not a theorem. Run python3 scripts/approach.py and keep the generic "specify a lemma" sentence out of the queue. Do not schedule a range extension that the campaign blocker already places inside a cited theorem.
  6. Exact probe — add a module to scripts/probes/ (search plus an independent checker, ideally on an existing engine), register it, add tamper tests, and wire it to a campaign and reproduced_known milestone. See "Add a probe" in LAB.md. Building an engine from the roadmap unlocks a whole class of problems.
  7. Pages improvements — submit accessible changes in docs/, keep all browser assets local, and use the generated snapshot rather than an independently maintained data copy.

Evidence standard#

An arXiv upload, press release, model-generated manuscript, or a proof assistant transcript does not alone establish acceptance of the full claim. Always compare precise quantifiers, conditions, and the encoded theorem.

  • official_open: exact target listed open by an official authority.
  • verified_open: contemporary primary specialist sources verify open remainder.
  • needs_review: still requires a proper source and scope audit.
  • claim_needs_review: potentially relevant proof/counterexample claim pending assessment.

Any proposed promotion to verified status should link the primary evidence and explain why restricted results or recent claim releases do not already settle it.

Research contributions checklist#

  • [ ] Exact claim and quantified domain documented
  • [ ] Strongest relevant existing theorem cited and dated
  • [ ] New assertion separated from known/reproduced cases
  • [ ] Proof checked independently or certificate replayed
  • [ ] Failed candidates and applicable resource limits reported
  • [ ] Implications for score justified, not inferred from publicity

Use the GitHub issue templates to propose evidence reviews or proof campaigns. See research protocol, source intake, and Pages guide.

Lean contributions#

Follow the Lean proof pipeline. Register exact theorem exports and scope gaps in data/lean_targets.json; run python3 scripts/lean_verify.py. A kernel check is evidence for the encoded statement, not automatic novelty or full-problem resolution.
