Yield — independently accepted results per unit of resource

Generated by python3 scripts/outcomes.py from data/outcomes.json. Do not edit by hand.

Capacity is how many claimable slots exist (SCALE.md). Yield is how many claimed items ended in a result that a named reviewer, who is not the author, accepted, per 10 recorded agent-hours. A merged artifact without independent review is pending, not a result.

Recording an outcome#

When a claim closes, append one record to data/outcomes.json in the PR that closes it:

{"work_id": "F-M079", "scope_sha256": "<first 16+ hex of the claimed digest>",
 "author": "<who did the work>", "outcome": "merged_pending_review",
 "claim_url": "https://github.com/Svyable/100/issues/<n>", "pr_url": "https://github.com/Svyable/100/pull/<n>",
 "claimed_on": "2026-11-01", "closed_on": "2026-11-02",
 "resources": {"agent_hours": 1.5, "ci_minutes": 6}}

Only a reviewer who is not the author may later change it to accepted_independent, adding reviewer and a public review_url. Report agent-hours honestly, including failed attempts; a gap_reported or falsified record is useful evidence and keeps the denominator truthful.
