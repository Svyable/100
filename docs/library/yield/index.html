Yield — independently accepted work items per unit of resource

Generated by python3 scripts/outcomes.py from data/outcomes.json. Do not edit by hand.

Capacity is how many claimable slots exist (SCALE.md). Yield is how many claimed work items a named reviewer, who is not the author, accepted, per 10 recorded agent-hours. A merged artifact without independent review is pending.

An accepted work item is not a new theorem. Comparators, status audits and reproductions can be accepted. A record counts as a new restricted mathematical result only with a separately audited novelty: new_restricted_result classification (its own named novelty reviewer and public link); that number is reported on its own line and is the only one comparable to the numerator of the preregistered benchmark (benchmarks/PROTOCOL.md).

Independence is structural: the validator checks that the named reviewer is not the author and that a public review link exists. It does not authenticate identity or expertise, and the proof, novelty and Solved gates remain separate.

Recording an outcome#

When a claim closes, append one record to data/outcomes.json in the PR that closes it:

{"work_id": "F-M079", "scope_sha256": "<first 16+ hex of the claimed digest>",
 "author": "<who did the work>", "outcome": "merged_pending_review",
 "claim_url": "https://github.com/Svyable/100/issues/<n>", "pr_url": "https://github.com/Svyable/100/pull/<n>",
 "claimed_on": "2026-11-01", "closed_on": "2026-11-02",
 "resources": {"agent_hours": 1.5, "ci_minutes": 6}}

Only a reviewer who is not the author may later change it to accepted_independent, adding reviewer, a public review_url and accepted_on. A separate novelty audit may then add novelty, novelty_reviewer and novelty_review_url. Report agent-hours honestly, as finite numbers, including failed attempts; a gap_reported or falsified record is useful evidence and keeps the denominator truthful.
