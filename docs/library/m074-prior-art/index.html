M074 — external fixed-outdegree-six proof claim: verification docket

Intake: 2026-10-31. Disposition: substantial external proof claim, not independently accepted here. Do not market the fixed-r=6 case as a fresh, unclaimed result without examining this work.

Exact claim#

The project Josh P., Fixed outdegree six in the Caccetta–Häggkvist conjecture claims that every finite loopless simple digraph with minimum outdegree at least 6 has a directed cycle of length at most ceil(n/6). It does not claim the unrestricted conjecture for all r.

The repository calls itself an unpublished computer-assisted proof that has not been peer reviewed. Version 0.2.0 and the cited archived DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21495432 identify the package. A separate 2026 computer search wustep/maths Caccetta–Häggkvist record reports further finite SAT exclusions. Neither independent repo should be treated as a journal acceptance.

Two independent kinds of verification#

  1. Computational replay: fetch the pinned source, reproduce the archive SHA-256 and all frozen CNF/proof hashes, and run the upstream quick/full verification. Capture replay outputs, exit status, tool versions, and hardware requirements.
  2. Mathematical audit: independently read the original theorems P1–P4, test graph-to-CNF implication, justify every branch reduction, inspect the n=18 regularity and symmetry arguments, and check the n=54 contradiction. A proof checker cannot establish the correctness of a hand-written mathematical reduction that produced its formula.

An independent audit should test deliberately corrupted proof data as a negative control. A successful archive hash alone does not establish the mathematical theorem. A successful proof replay does not automatically imply that the manuscript has been peer reviewed.

Local complement: two genuinely independent CNF formulations#

The direct minimum-outdegree encoder and compact exact-outdegree encoder encode different arc-assignment spaces but search for the same existence of a counterexample.

Every minimum-outdegree-r counterexample contains an exact-outdegree-r spanning subgraph: delete surplus outgoing arcs. Deleting arcs cannot create a directed 2-cycle, 3-cycle or any shorter cycle. Therefore satisfiability is equivalent, although a satisfying assignment in one encoding is not literally the same SAT variable assignment as in the other.

The compact representation has 13.4× fewer clauses, but may or may not solve faster. It includes new auxiliary prefix-threshold variables. A smaller CNF is not a proof and is not a guaranteed runtime improvement.

The compact gate recurrence is z[i,j] <=> z[i-1,j] OR (x[i] AND z[i-1,j-1]). Its four CNF implications are truth-table tested. The final constraints z[m,r] and NOT z[m,r+1] require exactly r outgoing arcs. Every arc assignment in the bounded test cases is independently checked against the expected graph semantics. The prefix completion is unique because each gate is a logical equivalence, so projecting away auxiliary variables leaves exactly the intended arc assignments.

Commands:

python3 scripts/ch_compact.py --n 18 --r 6 --output /tmp/ch18_compact.cnf
sha256sum /tmp/ch18_direct.cnf /tmp/ch18_compact.cnf

Review decision tree#

  • Full independent proof established for fixed r=6: record the restricted result and supporting independent reviews; continue targeting the unrestricted CH conjecture and non-overlapping methods. The Solved archive remains empty for M074.
  • Unresolved hand-reduction gap: document its exact location, construct the smallest failing example or missing lemma, and retain the claim as unverified.
  • Insufficient replay resources: log the missing proof asset/checker explicitly. No proof promotion.
  • Independent finite result: record its exact n,r scope and certificate identity; do not infer an all-n or all-r theorem.

Priority: independent review of the external claim outranks a fresh, unverified duplicate search at (18,6).
