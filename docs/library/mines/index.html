M019 · Erdos-Straus residue patterns for primes 1 mod 24#

Every prime n <= 8000 with n = 1 (mod 24) has an explicit positive solution of 4/n = 1/x + 1/y + 1/z, grouped by the residue 4x - n.

Frontier. Classical identities leave n = 1 (mod 24) uncovered. The table clusters one explicit solution per such prime in a finite range. It is not an identity and not a proof.

Nine polynomial identities give explicit 4/n = 1/x+1/y+1/z solutions on named arithmetic progressions inside n = 1 (mod 24); prime coverage is reported through 8000.

Frontier. Classical Mordell-type identities already cover many arithmetic progressions inside n = 1 (mod 24). M078 · Deck invariants that separate the census#

On the complete graph census for 3 <= n <= 6, the excess of graphs over classes of the deck-certified degree sequence, over degree sequence plus triangle count, and over those two plus the induced P4 count.

Frontier. Reconstruction is already verified by computer through at least 11 vertices (McKay 1997). Degree + triangles + induced P4 separates the census through 6 vertices here; that finite separation is not a general reconstruction proof.

M079 · Posets attaining balance exactly 1/3#

Every non-chain poset on at most 6 elements has best balance at least 1/3, and the width-height profile of those whose best balance is exactly 1/3 is the recorded multiset.

Frontier. The conjecture is verified through 11 elements (Peczarski) and proved for width 2 (Linial 1984). The table names the extremal posets inside this census.
