M094 — Hadamard existence frontier corrected (November 2, 2026)

Status correction, not a new mathematical result. The repository previously said that order 668 was the smallest multiple of four for which no Hadamard matrix was known. That was historically true after order 428 was settled in 2005, but it became false in August 2026.

Primary specialist source: Ronan Egan, Ilias S. Kotsireas, Stephen London and Pádraig Ó Catháin, Recent progress in the construction of Hadamard matrices, submitted 19 October 2026. The authors verify the twelve matrices announced on 12 August 2026 by Alpöge, Voinov and Reynolds-Haertle, including order 668, by exact multiplication of their ±1 matrices. They also construct sixteen additional orders and report that every multiple of four below 2000 has a known matrix. Their current catalogue lists 95 orders ≤10,000 without known constructions; the smallest is 2396 = 4×599 (see their introduction, Section 3 and Section 4).

Do not confuse three boundaries. The project’s implementation still lacks a constructor at order 188; this is not an open existence case. The specialist literature’s smallest currently unconstructed order is 2396. The unrestricted Hadamard conjecture remains open, and neither the August matrix announcement nor this October verification resolves it. These finite existence results are not this project's discoveries or independently accepted new work. The repository’s evidence and Solved states remain unchanged.

Falsifier: a contemporary primary construction or a correction to the specialist catalogue that changes the 2396 frontier; re-audit the date and scope before updating it again. The October 2026 source is a preprint with explicit independently checked finite matrices, not a peer-reviewed global conjecture proof.
