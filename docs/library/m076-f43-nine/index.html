RESEARCH DOSSIERS

M076 — sharp nine-deletion stability of the 54-set sunflower family

Date: 2026-11-02 UTC. Claim: #217. Work ID: X-M076-F43-NINE. Scope SHA-256: f11f4860e2b1b166b9a5426d1ffbfcac811bd69b8a7aa48bca7dd1ed83e63ed5.

Classification: author-derived, exact, restricted theorem candidate. Two complete cover enumerators, a separate literal-set orbit-free sweep, and exhaustive equality-pattern verification of simultaneous insertions agree. These are same-author checks, not independent mathematical review. Historical novelty is unestablished. This does not improve a global sunflower bound or solve the Erdős–Rado conjecture.

Exact statement and sharpness#

Let U={0,...,17} consist of two disjoint nine-point components, each partitioned into three blocks of size three. Let F consist of all four-sets taking two points from each of two different blocks in one component. Thus |F|=2×3×3×3=54. A three-petal sunflower means three distinct sets with all three pairwise intersections equal, including the empty intersection.

Nine-deletion theorem candidate. For every ambient set V⊇U, every D⊆F with |D|≤9, and every collection of distinct I⊆binom(V,4)\\F, if (F\\D)∪I contains no three-sunflower, then

|I| ≤ 4.

This is sharp at nine deletions: delete the old base_family() indices {0,1,9,10,11,18,21,25,26} and insert

{0,1,3,6}, {0,1,3,7}, {0,1,4,8}, {0,1,5,8}.

The resulting 49 distinct four-sets pass a literal check of all binom(49,3)=18,424 triples. This witness comes from the prior eight-deletion sharp example with one additional deletion; sharpness is inherited, not a new global lower-bound construction. A local 55-set improvement after nine deletions would require at least ten insertions. The candidate excludes even five.

Complete finite proof certificate#

  1. Automorphisms and ambient reduction. Permutations inside the three-point blocks, permutations of the three blocks of each component, exchange of the components, and arbitrary permutations of outside labels preserve F. A four-set's orbit is determined by its number of outside points and the unordered pair of sorted block-occupancy triples. The existing orbit classifier gives exactly 18 types (8, 5, 3, 1, 1 for 0–4 outsiders). The separate orbit-free 22-point frozenset sweep checks all binom(22,4)−54=7,261 alternatives, without using an orbit key to decide admissibility.
  1. Fifteen orbit exclusions. For any new candidate C, construct a graph H_C on the 54 old members, joining i,j precisely when F_i,F_j,C form a sunflower. Every deletion set allowing C must be a vertex cover of H_C. In 15 of the 18 representative graphs, a deterministic matching contains at least ten pairwise vertex-disjoint edges, each rechecked by literal set intersections. Nine deletions cannot cover ten disjoint edges. The three remaining orbit types are

The third orbit first becomes feasible at this deletion threshold: exactly eight nine-covers permit its representative. Those eight are already among the outside-type representative covers. The literal 22-point sweep finds exactly 630 labeled candidates (162+36 inside and 108×4 outside completions), normalizing to precisely the 306 patterns in the table.

  1. All nine-covers, twice. For the three representative forbidden graphs, recursively branch on an uncovered edge, pruning only when a disjoint matching exceeds the remaining deletion budget; expand every terminal cover to nine vertices. A second enumerator instead chooses one endpoint from each edge of a fixed disjoint matching, adds all remaining vertices, and tests all edges. The first two cover sets overlap in 712 patterns; all eight third-orbit covers are already in the second. Their union contains 878,401 distinct deletion masks relative to the fixed orbit representatives. This is not the number of all labeled nine-deletion subsets globally. Completeness follows because any admissible candidate can be conjugated to one of the three representatives; its conjugated deletion set must be in this union.

  1. Potential insertion capacity. For each of the 878,401 deletion masks, test all 306 normalized candidate graphs. An inside four-set contributes at most one insertion. A one-outsider support triple T contributes at most one completion if an old set containing T survives (that old set and two completions would form a sunflower), and at most two otherwise (three completions form a sunflower with core T). Therefore only 131 cover masks could possibly admit five insertions; none has capacity above six.

  1. Exhaustive simultaneous-insertion obstruction. For each of those 131 masks, independently reconstruct the surviving old sets as literal frozenset objects. Independently verify old-old-new compatibility for every individually allowed candidate. Form the finite list of all available insertion slots (one per inside candidate, one or two per outside support triple). Enumerate all 386 five-slot subsets. For outside slots, enumerate all equality partitions of outsider labels up to renaming (the Bell number for six slots is 203), rejecting duplicate labels for two completions of the same triple. Exactly 1,118 admissible equality-pattern instances are checked. In each, some new-new-old or new-new-new triple is a sunflower. Thus five simultaneous insertions are impossible. Any hypothetical larger insertion set contains a five-subset, so at most four are possible. The sharp witness establishes equality. For fewer than nine deletions, enlarge D to nine old sets; admissibility only becomes easier.

No SAT/ILP solver result, randomized search, or global classification of all 54-member sunflower-free families is assumed.

The complete check uses the two cover enumeration algorithms, all 18 orbit graphs and ten-disjoint-pair obstructions, the orbit-free literal 22-point sweep, the 878,401-mask capacity census, the 131-mask simultaneous-insertion falsifier search, and a literal sharpness check. Adversarial tests include forged covers, invalid five-set augmentation, partition completeness, and the new third-orbit threshold.

Failed avenues and corrections: The natural extension of the eight-deletion proof that only the (2,1,1) and one-outsider (2,1,0) orbits matter is false: (3,1,0) becomes possible after nine deletions. The naive individual-capacity bound is six, not four, so it does not prove the theorem; explicit new-new-old and new-new-new incompatibility checks are essential. No five-set insertion was found in the complete 131-mask residual search. The search is exact within its stated exhaustive reduction, not a heuristic failure to find a witness.

Next falsifiers: A ten-edge matching that is not disjoint or is not genuinely forbidden; a missed orbit; a missing nine-cover in either enumeration; a capacity-six mask with five compatible new sets under an untested outsider equality pattern; a sunflower in the 49-set witness; or a historical theorem already implying this specific local bound. The ten-deletion neighborhood is not classified. A new independent orbit/action checker, formalized small-cover lemma, and external specialist review of the 1972–2026 finite Δ-system literature are appropriate next steps.
