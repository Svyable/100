Verified milestones

Reproductions of established mathematics test the machinery; they are not new theorems.

  • REPRODUCED KNOWN · 2026-10-31Low zeros of L(s, chi_4) and L(s, chi_3) are simple and on the critical line

    Argument counts matched to sign changes: chi_4 on (6, 7) and (10, 11) gives N(11) = 2, and chi_3 on (8, 9) gives N(9) = 1. Gauss sums tau(chi_3)^2 = -3 and tau(chi_4) = 2i fix the root numbers. The deep run adds chi_4 to N(17) = 4, chi_3 to N(15) = 2, and the Legendre symbol mod 5 to N(12) = 3, with tau(chi_5)^2 = 5.

    Scope: Two real characters at classical heights; not the generalized Riemann hypothesis

    python3 scripts/lab.py --probe dirichlet_l
  • REPRODUCED KNOWN · 2026-11-01GRH to height 20 for the real primitive characters of conductor 3, 4 and 5

    For D = -4, -3, 5, L(s, chi_D) has no real zero in (0, 1) (Lipschitz-certified point values), the argument principle certifies 5, 4 and 6 zeros with 0 < Im s < 20, and Z(t, chi_D) has exactly as many certified sign changes. The checker rebuilds each character and certifies the root number +1 from the Gauss sum. The deep run covers all 17 real primitive characters of conductor at most 24 to height 30 (248 zeros in the upper half-plane).

    Scope: Known small range; not new
