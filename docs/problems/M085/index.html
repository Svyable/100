The Unique Games Theorem ↗ (2026-09-23)
Claimed theorem
For any fixed epsilon,delta in (0,1/2), a polynomial-time reduction from 3SAT to Unique Games over a fixed finite alphabet has completeness at least 1-epsilon and soundness at most delta.
Scope vs. registry
Matches the registry statement A uniform, valid hardness reduction with those quantifiers would resolve the registered Unique Games conjecture as normally formulated.
