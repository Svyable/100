Exact rational LP with duality certificates over graphic metrics

Hypothesis
For every metric TSP instance the optimal tour costs at most 4/3 of the Held-Karp (subtour LP) bound.
First proof obligation
Reproduce published exact maximum integrality gaps for all small metrics (Benoit-Boyd) with LP-duality certificates, then target a structured class such as graphic metrics of subcubic graphs beyond published ranges.
Next executable step
Extend from graphic metrics to 1-2 and vertex-weighted metrics, and enumerate extreme points of the subtour polytope at n = 8 to search for gap-maximising instances.
What would falsify it
A metric instance with an exactly certified Held-Karp value and a brute-force optimal tour longer than 4/3 of it.
Current blocker
Exhaustive extreme-point enumeration grows very quickly beyond n = 10; graphic metrics up to 7 vertices reach only a 16/15 gap.
