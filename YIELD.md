# Yield — independently accepted work items per unit of resource

Generated by `python3 scripts/outcomes.py` from [data/outcomes.json](data/outcomes.json). Do not edit by hand.

Capacity is how many claimable slots exist ([SCALE.md](SCALE.md)). **Yield** is how many claimed work items a named reviewer, who is not the author, accepted, per 10 recorded agent-hours. A merged artifact without independent review is *pending*.

**An accepted work item is not a new theorem.** Comparators, status audits and reproductions can be accepted. A record counts as a *new restricted mathematical result* only with a separately audited `novelty: new_restricted_result` classification (its own named novelty reviewer and public link); that number is reported on its own line and is the only one comparable to the numerator of the preregistered benchmark ([benchmarks/PROTOCOL.md](benchmarks/PROTOCOL.md)).

> Independence is structural: the validator checks that the named reviewer is not the author and that a public review link exists. It does not authenticate identity or expertise, and the proof, novelty and Solved gates remain separate.

## Outcomes

| Outcome | Count | Meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| `accepted_independent` | 0 | Delivered and accepted by a named reviewer who is not the author. |
| `merged_pending_review` | 0 | Artifact merged; independent review not yet recorded. |
| `gap_reported` | 0 | A precise gap, obstruction or negative result was recorded instead of the deliverable. |
| `falsified` | 0 | The deliverable's claim was refuted, or its falsifier was found. |
| `abandoned` | 0 | Claim closed without a usable artifact. |

## Recording an outcome

When a claim closes, append one record to `data/outcomes.json` in the PR that closes it:

```json
{"work_id": "F-M079", "scope_sha256": "<first 16+ hex of the claimed digest>",
 "author": "<who did the work>", "outcome": "merged_pending_review",
 "claim_url": "https://github.com/Svyable/100/issues/<n>", "pr_url": "https://github.com/Svyable/100/pull/<n>",
 "claimed_on": "2026-11-01", "closed_on": "2026-11-02",
 "resources": {"agent_hours": 1.5, "ci_minutes": 6}}
```

Only a reviewer who is not the author may later change it to `accepted_independent`, adding `reviewer`, a public `review_url` and `accepted_on`. A separate novelty audit may then add `novelty`, `novelty_reviewer` and `novelty_review_url`. Report agent-hours honestly, as finite numbers, including failed attempts; a `gap_reported` or `falsified` record is useful evidence and keeps the denominator truthful.
