### M078 · Deck invariants that separate the census

On the complete graph census for 3 <= n <= 7, excess over the degree sequence, over degree sequence plus triangles, and over the deck of card degree sequences plus triangles.

**Frontier.** Reconstruction is already verified by computer through at least 11 vertices (McKay 1997). The new column is the multiset of card degree sequences, read from the deck. ### M079 · Posets attaining balance exactly 1/3

**Frontier.** The conjecture is verified through 11 elements (Peczarski) and proved for width 2 (Linial 1984). The table names the extremal posets inside this census.
