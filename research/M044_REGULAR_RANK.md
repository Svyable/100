# M044 finite group-ring left-regular rank cross-check

**Claim:** [#332](https://github.com/Svyable/100/issues/332). Scope digest: `X-M044-REGULAR-RANK|finite-F2-C3-S3|left-regular-rank|v1`.

**Source and exact scope:** Standard elementary regular-representation identity: for a finite-dimensional unital algebra over a field, left multiplication by `a` is invertible as a linear map precisely when `a` is a unit. The finite group-algebra definition and source provenance are recorded in [M044_GROUP_RING_CORE.md](M044_GROUP_RING_CORE.md), based on the M044 manuscript/type-parity audit. This is an author-side cross-check of the arithmetic from [PR #330](https://github.com/Svyable/100/pull/330), not independent human review or a new theorem.

**Methods:** `scripts/m044_regular_rank.py` constructs each left-regular matrix column directly from group-basis products, computes binary rank with packed-column Gaussian elimination, and independently enumerates two-sided inverses with the preexisting convolution implementation. It checks every element of F2[C3] and F2[S3], including zero, identity, singular dependent columns and out-of-range controls. The exhaustive census is **3 units / 5 nonunits** in F2[C3] and **12 units / 52 nonunits** in F2[S3]. These counts are finite reproductions and can be falsified by any mask for which full rank disagrees with inverse existence.

**Reproduction:** `python3 scripts/m044_regular_rank.py` and `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p 'test_m044_regular_rank.py'`. Python standard library only. The matrix method does not call the convolution routine; the inverse-enumeration comparison does. Both share the finite Cayley tables. No claim is made about an arbitrary group presentation, torsion-free groups, direct finiteness beyond finite rings, or the missing M044 matching-graph witness. A decisive next falsifier is a source-pinned nontrivial group presentation and concrete words, checked by independent word-reduction engines. No solved or proof-obligation status changes; no independent mathematical acceptance.
