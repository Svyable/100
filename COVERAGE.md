## Guardrails

All candidate packets come from the existing dependency-gated work queue, including prior-art refusals and scope hashes. The planner does not invent claim IDs, change scores or solved states, declare a paper independently reviewed, or reclassify open problems. Actual progress is accounted for by `data/outcomes.json` and independently reviewed claims, not by these planned coverage counts.
